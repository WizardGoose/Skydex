# Skydex 1.1.1

Fixed pet icons appearing too small and off-centre in Profile's Gear card.
The correction applies to every pet and custom skin, including the inactive
pet placeholder, and keeps the compact Pets-row sizing within its own layout.

Removed and unreleased accessories, including Compass Talisman, Eternal Crystal,
and Luck Talisman, are hidden from the accessory catalogue and its progress counts.
Admin-only Master Skull tiers 8–10 and Ring of Space are also excluded.
Retired event variants such as old Crab and Sloth Hats are hidden from the progression
lists. Existing owned items still contribute to the profile's Magical Power.
Fixed the accessory filter panel being clipped by the surrounding card.

The companion mod now includes Skydex's icon in its Fabric metadata, so launchers
such as Prism can display it in their mod list. Prism's provider remains Unknown
for manually installed GitHub downloads.

The website fix is available at [skydex.ca](https://skydex.ca). To get the launcher
icon, replace the existing Skydex JAR with the matching 1.1.1 download below.
The mod supports Fabric on Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2, with the matching Fabric API.
