# Skydex 1.1.2

Fixed Slayer levels getting stuck at 6 when Hypixel records higher claimed
rewards with a special suffix. This also fixes the incorrect next-level XP
target, such as Revenant showing `1.2M / 100K XP` instead of level 9 and
`1.2M XP` at max level.

The shared correction applies to Profile, the public Profile Viewer, and
Slayer requirements in Recipes and Accessories. Unclaimed rewards still do
not count as unlocked levels.

The fix is available at [skydex.ca](https://skydex.ca). Refresh the website
and profile data to update the displayed levels. No mod update is needed for
this website fix. The companion mod remains at 1.1.1; its existing downloads
for Fabric on Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2 are included unchanged.
